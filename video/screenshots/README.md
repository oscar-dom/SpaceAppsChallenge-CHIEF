# Video screenshots

The screenshots of the app used in the demo video, as backgrounds under the animated CHIEF
([`../chief-animator/`](../chief-animator/README.md)).

## Conventions

- **Size:** PNG, 16:9 (the video is 1920 × 1080).
- **Names:** numbered in the order they appear in the video, with a short description:
  `01-crew-overview.png`, `02-member-overview.png`, `03-alerts.png`, `04-mission-route.png`,
  `05-inventory.png`.
- **Data:** the app's simulated data only. Every screen already carries the "Simulated data"
  label and the prototype disclaimer; keep them visible.

To frame CHIEF over a screenshot, pick *App screenshot…* as the preview background in the
animator.
